## About

**CodesAngel** is a community of developers, designers, and problem-solvers who believe great software should be **open, elegant, and built to last**.
We design and ship tools, products, and experiments, and we share everything we learn along the way.

> [!NOTE]
> *"Every line of code is a chance to make someone's day a little easier."*

## Engineering Principles

| Principle | What it means |
|:--|:--|
| **Quality** | Clean code, clear documentation, and meaningful tests. |
| **Privacy** | Local-first by design. Your data stays on your device. |
| **Transparency** | Open discussions, public roadmaps, and versioned releases. |
| **Modularity** | Small, reusable components that are easy to maintain. |

## Contributing

We welcome contributions of every size, from bug reports and documentation to new features.

1. **Find an issue.** Browse [open issues](https://github.com/search?q=org%3ACodesAngel+is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen&type=issues) across our repositories.
2. **Fork and branch.** Review the project's README and contribution guidelines before you start.
3. **Submit a pull request.** Describe what you changed and why.
4. **Collaborate.** Respond to review feedback and help us refine the change.

> First time contributing to open source? Open an issue with your question, and a maintainer will help you get started.
